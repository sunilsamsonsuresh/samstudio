How to Reach Us

Follow these step-by-step directions to find your way to Rahsun Estates

1

Start from Perumalmalai Check Post

From Perumalmalai check post, head towards Palani road for approximately 1.5 km until you reach the Pethuparai pirivu diversion.

2

Take the Downlane at Pethuparai Pirivu

At Pethuparai pirivu, take the left downlane. Continue on this route for about 3-4 km until you reach a river bridge. Look for the Spice Valley and Elephant Valley signboards.

3

Property Entrance

You will see a blue gate with a sign "This Property Belongs to Suprabharath Exports Private Limited".

4

Final Approach to Rahsun Estates

Continue to the second blue gate - this is the vehicle entry point for Rahsun Estates.

Important: When you reach the second blue gate, please honk your horn. Our staff will come to open the gate for you.
